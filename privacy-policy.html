CityFood Privacy Policy

Food Delivery Application
Effective Date: August 17, 2026 | Last Updated: August 17, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CityFood ("we," "our," or "us"). We operate the CityFood food delivery application (the "App") available on Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By using the CityFood App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this policy, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name - Your full name for order processing and delivery
  • Phone Number - For delivery coordination and order updates
  • Email Address - For account creation and communication
  • Delivery Address - Your physical address for order delivery

2.2 Location Data

  • GPS Location - Collected only when you place an order, used for delivery purposes
  • Location Permission - Required for real-time order tracking and finding nearby restaurants

2.3 Order Information

  • Order history and preferences
  • Payment method (Cash on Delivery only)
  • Restaurant and item selections

2.4 Device Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • Unique device identifiers

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Processing and delivering your food orders
  • Providing real-time order tracking
  • Communicating order status updates and notifications
  • Processing payments (Cash on Delivery)
  • Improving our services and user experience
  • Providing customer support
  • Ensuring safety and fraud prevention

4. Information Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information with:

  • Restaurants - To process your food orders (name, phone, delivery address)
  • Delivery Riders - To deliver your orders (name, phone, delivery address)
  • Firebase Services - For data storage and authentication (Google LLC)

5. Location Data Usage

We collect GPS location data only for:

  • Delivering orders to your specified address
  • Displaying nearby restaurants and stores
  • Real-time order tracking during delivery
Note: You can disable location services at any time in your device settings. However, some features may not work properly without location access.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmission
  • Firebase secure backend infrastructure
  • Limited access to personal information
  • Regular security audits

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. Order history is maintained for 7 days for settlement and record-keeping purposes. You can request data deletion by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

CityFood is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete such information.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

10. Third-Party Services

Our App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google LLC) - For authentication, database, and notifications
  • OpenStreetMap - For map services (open-source, no personal data collected)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
